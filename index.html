About Me

I'm Abraham Judah Cephas — a civil servant in Government of India and currently a Master's candidate at UC Berkeley's Goldman School of Public Policy (2025–2027). My focus is AI safety and governance, technology policy, and development economics.

I spent a decade at the Comptroller and Auditor General of India auditing digital government systems, uncovering procurement fraud, and working at the intersection of data, accountability, and public institutions. My research now asks: what happens when the state delegates decisions to algorithms?

Outside of work, I follow economics obsessively, play tennis more than I probably should, and try to write clearly about complicated things.
